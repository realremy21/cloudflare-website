Why Springs panels need looking after

Colorado Springs stacks up the exact conditions that quietly cost solar owners production:

  • Dust and pollen. Wind off the plains and spring pollen season coat panels in a film rain won't remove.
  • Pigeons. The space under an array is prime nesting habitat, and a nesting colony is hard on wiring, roofing, and your sleep.
  • Hail and storms. Springs sits in hail country. After a major storm, a documented visual inspection and photo record is what turns "I think it's fine" into something you can hand an insurer.
  • Snow. Heavy, wet spring snow can sit on panels for days. Ask us about safe options — never climb a snowy roof or scrape panels with tools that can crack glass.
