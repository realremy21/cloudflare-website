Colorado Springs solar cleaning questions

How often should solar panels be cleaned in Colorado Springs?

Most systems should be checked seasonally and cleaned when dust, pine pollen, bird droppings, or hail-season residue are visible. Flat or low-slope arrays may need attention more often.

Do you charge a trip fee for Colorado Springs?

No. We batch Colorado Springs work into weekly Springs Days, so Springs homes pay the same published flat rates as our Denver metro customers — no trip fee and no travel surcharge.

Can you check for pigeons during a cleaning?

Yes. We look for visible nesting material, droppings, and debris under and around the array and document what we can safely see with photos. If pigeons have moved in, our critter guard service handles removal, cleanup, sanitizing, and sealing.

Do you provide before and after photos?

Yes. Photo proof is part of the maintenance value: it helps show the cleaning result and creates a clearer record of visible roofline or panel-area concerns.
