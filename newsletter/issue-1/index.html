Holiday lights

The holiday season is around the corner, and honestly, this is one of my favorite stretches of the year. Our Christmas and holiday light installs are some of the most fun work we do — there’s something genuinely good about helping a family make their home a little brighter for the holidays. If lights are on your mind, now’s the time to reach out before the calendar fills up.

Fall is also the right moment for the unglamorous-but-important stuff: window cleaning and gutter cleaning before winter settles in. Clean glass, clear gutters, and one less thing to worry about when the snow comes.

If lights, windows, or gutters are on your fall list, just use our contact page and we’ll get you on the schedule.

Know what normal looks like

Before winter settles in, take a quick look at your solar monitoring and get familiar with what your system is producing.

Seasonal changes are normal. The point isn’t to worry about every dip—it’s to know your system well enough to recognize when something looks genuinely unusual.

Shade, dust, pollen, bird droppings, debris, and changing site conditions can all affect what you see.
