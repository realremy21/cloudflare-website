Why Arvada panels get dirty their own way

  • Plains-edge wind. West Arvada subdivisions sit exposed. Wind-blown dust and construction grit from still-growing neighborhoods settle on panels and bake on under high-altitude sun.
  • Mature canopy in the east. Older Arvada's cottonwoods and pines drop pollen and organic residue that sticks, especially along the lower edge of each panel where it does the most harm.
  • Hail-alley weather. Arvada takes real hail. Storm seasons leave grit, splashed debris at array edges, and occasionally damage that only shows up when someone is on the roof looking — a cleaning after storm season doubles as that look.
  • Low-slope new roofs. Much of the west-side solar went onto the low pitches of newer two-stories. Low tilt rinses poorly, so each season's film becomes the next season's base layer.

Rain doesn't fix any of this. It redistributes loose dust, spot-dries into mineral marks, and leaves the bonded material — pollen, droppings, edge residue — untouched.

How we clean: the warranty-safe way

Panel manufacturers are specific about what can touch their glass, and we work the way the manuals say:

  • Pure water — no minerals left behind to spot or film the glass as it dries.
  • Panel-safe soft brushes — no abrasives, nothing that scratches the anti-reflective coating your production depends on.
  • No harsh chemicals — manufacturer-friendly, and nothing running off your roof that you'd mind in your yard.
  • No pressure washers, ever — high-pressure water damages seals and can drive moisture where it doesn't belong.

Done right, a cleaning is gentle, thorough, and leaves you photos proving the difference. Done wrong, it can void the warranty on a system worth tens of thousands of dollars. That gap is the whole reason we exist.

Arvada cleaning questions

How often should panels be cleaned in Arvada?

Once or twice a year covers most Arvada systems: after spring pollen, and again after a dusty or stormy summer. Exposed west-Arvada arrays and low-slope roofs collect faster and often justify the twice-yearly rhythm; your production trend is the real guide.

Will cleaning void my panel warranty?

Cleaning it wrong can — manufacturers prohibit abrasive tools, harsh chemicals, and pressure washing. Our method (pure water, soft panel-safe brushes, no chemicals) is the approach the manuals approve.
