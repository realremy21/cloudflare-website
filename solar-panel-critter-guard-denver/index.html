Critter Guard Questions

Solar panel bird guard FAQ

What is solar panel critter guard?

Critter guard is a protective mesh barrier installed around the exposed edge of a solar array. It helps block birds and small animals from nesting beneath panels while keeping air flow around the system.

When should Denver homeowners consider bird guard?

It is worth checking when you see birds landing near the array, nesting material, droppings below the roofline, scratching sounds, or repeated debris collecting under panel edges.

Can critter guard be installed with a solar cleaning?

Yes. Cleaning and guard installation can often be coordinated in the same service window when access is safe and the array condition is ready for installation.

Does critter guard replace roof or electrical repair?

No. Critter guard is a prevention and maintenance service. If we see roof damage, exposed wiring concerns, or anything outside routine service scope, we document it so you can contact the right licensed trade.
