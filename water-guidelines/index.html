Our Water-Conscious Cleaning Approach

At Mile High Solar Care, water conservation isn’t an afterthought—it’s built into how we operate.

We use purified, controlled-flow cleaning systems that minimize water usage while maximizing solar panel performance. Our process is designed to be efficient, precise, and environmentally responsible.

For added flexibility and conservation, we also have the ability to bring our own onboard water supply to job sites. This allows us to complete both residential and commercial cleanings without relying on a customer’s water source when needed.

This approach helps

  • • Reduce unnecessary water consumption
  • • Avoid strain on residential water systems
  • • Ensure compliance with local guidelines
  • • Maintain consistent, high-quality results

We stay up to date with local water regulations across every city we serve and adjust our methods accordingly—so our customers never have to worry about compliance.
