Colorado Plug-In Solar FAQ

Questions to answer before buying or installing.

Is plug-in solar actually legal in Colorado?

Yes. A 2026 Colorado law (HB26-1007) makes small plug-in solar devices legal statewide — up to 1,920 watts, one system per home. Smaller kits (391 watts and under) plug into a standard outlet with no wiring changes. Your utility can’t require approval or charge a fee, and the law’s HOA and landlord protections fully phase in January 1, 2027.

I’m a renter. Can I really do this?

In most cases, yes. The law treats these kits as your personal property, like a piece of furniture — and the renter path is built on notifying your landlord, which we help you word. The kit is yours and moves with you when you do.

How much will it actually save me?

Honestly: a well-placed small kit in the Denver area produces roughly 400–650 kilowatt-hours a year — on the order of $65–$95 a year off your bill at typical rates, more if your spot is sunny and well-aimed. It’s a real offset for daytime use, not a whole-home replacement. We’ll tell you straight if your spot won’t produce enough to be worth it.

Why not just plug it in myself?

You can — the small tier is designed for it. Most people call us because the details decide whether it works: the right outlet and circuit, mounting that survives Colorado wind, cables that don’t become a trip hazard, the app actually paired, and proof it’s producing. We also check the kit meets Colorado’s listing rules before it goes on your home — and we’re in and out in about an hour.

Do plug-in solar projects need an electrician?

Not at 391 watts and under — that tier skips wiring requirements entirely. Anything involving wiring, circuits, outlets, panels, grounding, meter collars, or permits routes to a licensed electrician, and the biggest legal systems (up to 1,920 W) require one. We coordinate that lane and quote it in writing.

What do you need from me to book?

Your address, whether you rent or own, where the kit would go (a balcony or patio photo helps), and which kit you have — or want us to bring. That’s enough to confirm your setup is in the standard tier and get you scheduled.
