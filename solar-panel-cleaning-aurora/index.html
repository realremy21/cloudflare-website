Aurora Solar Questions

Solar cleaning and maintenance FAQ

How often should solar panels be cleaned in Aurora?

Most Aurora systems benefit from at least seasonal review, with cleaning scheduled when dust, bird debris, pollen, or runoff residue is visible. Homes near open space, trees, construction, or heavy bird activity may need more frequent service.

Does rain clean solar panels?

Rain helps rinse loose dust, but it often leaves edge buildup, mineral spotting, pollen streaks, and debris below panel frames. A panel-safe cleaning is more thorough than waiting for weather.

Can you check critter guard during an Aurora cleaning?

Yes. When access is safe, we can look at visible guard condition, loose edges, nesting signs, and debris around the array. If new guard is needed, we quote that separately from cleaning.

Do you provide before and after photos?

Yes. Photo proof is part of how we document completed cleaning, visible debris, access issues, and any maintenance notes that should be carried forward.
