Apex Solar Solutions Has Left Colorado — What Denver-Area Homeowners Should Do Next

By Jeremy & MeralPublished November 17, 2026

If your solar panels were installed by Apex Solar Solutions, you may have already discovered the problem: the phone doesn't reach anyone, and the email bounces. You're not imagining it.

What happened

Apex Solar Solutions was a Wyoming-incorporated company that registered to do business in Colorado in 2017 and grew into one of the more visible solar sales operations in the Denver metro, accumulating more than 200 Google reviews. According to the Colorado Secretary of State's business records, the company has withdrawn from Colorado — its record reads “Colorado Authority Relinquished April 13, 2026,” and its corporate status in Colorado is Withdrawn. Its published email address no longer exists, and its phone goes unanswered.

We are not affiliated with Apex Solar Solutions, and we can't honor their contracts or their warranties. What we can do is explain where their customers stand — and help.

What this means for your system

  • Your equipment warranties generally survive. Panel and inverter warranties come from the manufacturers, not from Apex. In most cases those remain valid — keep your paperwork.
  • Your service safety net is gone. Apex's own service promises, workmanship coverage, and monitoring support were Apex's obligations. With the company out of Colorado, there's no one on the other end.
  • Your system still needs care. Dirty panels lose production. Critters nest under arrays. Inverters age out. Monitoring quietly drops offline. None of that pauses because an installer left.

What to do next

  1. Find your install paperwork: contract, equipment list, warranty certificates, loan or lease documents.
  2. Check your monitoring app — is it reporting current production?
  3. Look at your production history, if you can see it. A slow decline usually means soiling or a failing component.
  4. Get the system looked at by a company that services systems it didn't install. (That's us — but whoever you choose, choose someone local who answers the phone.)
