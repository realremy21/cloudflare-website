Simple Terms

Plain language, no surprises

  • Cancel anytime. Monthly plans: no contract, no cancellation fee — cancel and you're done at the end of the paid month. Annual plans: pro-rated refund for unused months, minus cleanings already used at one-off rates.
  • No price hikes mid-term. Your rate is locked for 12 months from signup; we'll tell you 30 days before any change.
  • Scheduling: we contact you when a cleaning is due; you pick the window. Weather reschedules are free and automatic.
  • Discounts apply to MHSC labor and installs while the plan is active; they don't stack with other promotions.
  • Transfer: moving? The plan transfers to the new homeowner free — a genuine selling point for your listing.

What's not included

Electrical work or inverter repair (we're a non-electrical O&M crew — we'll diagnose, document, and refer), panel or roof replacement, structural roof work, snow removal, and manufacturer warranty claims (we document and support them, the manufacturer decides). Monitoring reviews use your existing Enphase/SolarEdge app access — we don't install monitoring hardware under these plans.
