What a commercial maintenance program includes

  • Scheduled panel cleaning. Pure water, panel-safe soft brushes, no harsh chemicals, no pressure washers — the method module manufacturers approve, on a cadence matched to your site (dust load, tilt, surroundings) instead of a generic calendar.
  • Documented inspections. Every panel photographed, findings written up in a report you keep: glass condition, mounting and racking, visible wiring, critter and debris signs. The same Inspection & Report discipline we sell residentially for $245, scaled to your array and priced per site.
  • Storm and hail documentation. Colorado hail season is not a hypothetical. After a storm event we document panel-by-panel condition with date-stamped photos and a written report — the record your adjuster, your warranty file, and your own board or owners actually need.
  • Critter exclusion. Pigeons and squirrels under a commercial array chew wiring and create fire and liability exposure. We install and repair critter guard and document the before/after.
  • Production sanity checks. We read your monitoring the way an owner would: if a string is down or production has stepped off a cliff, you hear it from us with photos attached — not from a quarterly utility report.

How pricing works

Commercial pricing is quoted per site, after a short review of array size, access, and the scope you want covered — cleaning only, cleaning plus inspection, or a full recurring program with storm response. The honest commercial case

Most commercial arrays in Colorado are not being maintained at all — they're being assumed. The installer who sold the system is not coming back to clean it, the monitoring alert goes to an inbox nobody owns, and the first real look at the array happens when production has been down long enough to hurt. If that's your situation, the cheapest first step is one documented inspection: you'll know exactly what you own, what it's doing, and what — if anything — it needs. Sometimes the answer is "a cleaning and a schedule." Sometimes it's "nothing yet." We'll tell you the truth either way, in writing, with photos.

Commercial maintenance questions

Both. One-off cleanings and inspections are how many commercial relationships start; most sites move to a recurring program once the first documented inspection shows what a schedule prevents. Programs are quoted per site with the cadence and scope in writing.

Can you provide a W-9, COI, and safety documentation for vendor onboarding?

Yes — W-9, certificate of insurance, and our written safety policy are available on request, and we're glad to complete your vendor packet before any work is scheduled.

What areas do you cover for commercial work?

The Denver metro and the Front Range — including Colorado Springs and southern Colorado for larger sites and fields. If a site is outside our normal routes we'll tell you plainly and, where we can, group it with other work to make the trip sensible.

How is commercial pricing different from your published residential pricing?

Residential services carry published flat prices (cleaning from $129, Inspection & Report $245, Electrical Diagnostic $295).
