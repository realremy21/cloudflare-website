Preferred site profile

Ground-mounted and safely accessible

  • Colorado ground-mounted solar array
  • 1P single-axis trackers preferred
  • Approximately 8 feet of inter-row spacing
  • At least 6 feet of operating clearance
  • Relatively flat, safely accessible travel path

Other tracker and racking configurations can be reviewed individually.
